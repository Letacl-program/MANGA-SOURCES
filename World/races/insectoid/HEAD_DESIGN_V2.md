# Insectoid — HEAD DESIGN V2

> **Status: APPROVED — V2 visual redesign**
>
> V2 replaces the previous visual head language defined in `HEAD_DESIGN.md`.
> The biological/worldbuilding documentation of the Insectoid race remains unchanged.
>
> **Core visual ratio: approximately 80% humanoid / 20% insectoid.**

## 1. Core visual concept

The Insectoid head should read primarily as:

> **A humanoid/anime character head with restrained insectoid characteristics.**

The target visual balance is approximately:

**80% humanoid — 20% insectoid**

This is a deliberate shift toward a more human-readable face.

The Insectoid should no longer look like a humanoid insect with an insect head. Instead, it should look like a humanoid character whose face and silhouette contain a small number of unmistakable insectoid traits.

The design must remain compatible with the **Cartoon** visual style used for the other races.

---

## 2. Human-readable facial structure

The basic facial proportions are strongly humanoidal.

The head should have:

- a recognizable humanoid face shape,
- human-like facial proportions,
- a clear forehead and cheek structure,
- a relatively narrow chin,
- a readable jawline,
- expressive facial geometry.

The face should remain attractive and approachable in the same general visual language as other humanoidal races.

The insectoid traits are layered onto this structure rather than replacing the entire face.

---

## 3. Eyes — revised V2 design

The eyes are the main insectoid feature.

They should be:

- large,
- clearly larger than typical human/anime eyes,
- almond-shaped and humanoid in silhouette,
- amber/orange by default in the reference design,
- visibly compound in texture,
- surrounded by a clearly visible **white sclera**.

### Critical V2 rule: visible sclera

The white of the eye must remain visible in **all standard views and expressions**.

The eyes should NOT become completely orange/compound surfaces when viewed from:

- front,
- three-quarter,
- profile,
- emotional expressions,
- color variants.

The front view establishes the reference appearance:

> **Human-readable eye shape + visible white sclera + insectoid compound iris/pupil texture.**

The compound structure is primarily expressed inside the colored iris rather than by replacing the entire visible eye with an insect-like compound sphere.

This is a key part of the 80/20 balance.

### Eye expression

Because the eyes retain a humanoid structure, they can communicate:

- neutral,
- curiosity,
- happiness,
- concentration,
- surprise,
- tiredness,
- aggression.

The eyes remain one of the strongest visual identifiers of the race, but they should no longer dominate the entire face.

---

## 4. Nose

The V2 design allows a **very subtle humanoid nose structure**.

It should be:

- small,
- simple,
- unobtrusive,
- integrated into the humanoid facial proportions.

The nose must not become strongly humanistic or detailed.

The goal is to support the 80/20 humanoid appearance without losing the alien/insectoid identity.

---

## 5. Mouth and jaw

The mouth remains the main visible insectoid anatomical feature.

### Neutral state

In the neutral state the lower face can appear almost humanoidal at first glance.

There is no prominent conventional human lip structure.

The insectoid jaw/mandible elements are integrated into the cheek and jaw silhouette.

### Open state

The jaw retains its defining insectoid capability:

> **It can open extremely widely.**

When fully opened, the insectoid anatomy becomes immediately apparent.

The opening can extend dramatically across the lower face, producing the characteristic exaggerated insectoid jaw.

The extreme opening should be reserved for:

- strong emotion,
- aggression,
- threat,
- alarm,
- dramatic expression,
- biological demonstration.

This creates a useful contrast:

**closed = ~80% humanoid**

**fully open = strongly insectoid**

---

## 6. Mandibles

The mandibles are simplified and integrated into the face.

They should:

- remain clearly insectoid,
- follow the humanoid cheek/jaw silhouette when closed,
- be relatively small and elegant in the neutral state,
- become much more prominent during wide opening,
- avoid realistic multi-part arthropod mouth anatomy.

The mandibles should be treated as a strong graphic feature rather than a detailed anatomical diagram.

---

## 7. Antennae

The head has two simple antennae.

V2 characteristics:

- two antennae,
- slender,
- relatively short,
- smooth and readable,
- simple segmented construction,
- small contrasting tips,
- expressive through position and movement.

They should frame the head without overwhelming it.

They are a secondary insectoid identifier, not the dominant feature.

---

## 8. Exoskeletal elements

The V2 head retains simplified exoskeletal elements, but they are much more restrained than in the earlier design.

Use:

- a few large plates,
- clean decorative divisions,
- subtle dark/contrasting areas around the temples, crown and sides,
- smooth transitions between skin-like humanoid areas and exoskeletal surfaces.

Avoid:

- dense plate networks,
- heavy insect carapace coverage,
- excessive segmentation,
- realistic arthropod head armor.

The overall impression should remain closer to a humanoid character with ornamental biological armor.

---

## 9. Rear and profile views

The front view establishes the primary 80/20 balance.

In profile and rear views the insectoid characteristics may become somewhat stronger.

### Profile

The profile should preserve:

- humanoid forehead,
- humanoid facial proportions,
- readable nose/chin silhouette,
- simplified mandible structure,
- compound eye with visible sclera,
- restrained exoskeletal side plates,
- two antennae.

### Rear

The rear view may show more of the exoskeletal structure because there is no human facial information competing with it.

However, the head should still remain simple and smooth.

---

## 10. Expressions

Expressions are primarily communicated through the humanoid facial structure.

Important channels:

1. eye shape and direction,
2. eyelid position,
3. antenna position,
4. mouth/jaw movement,
5. head tilt.

The V2 design should support normal humanoidal expressions without requiring the jaw to be opened.

Wide jaw opening is a special expressive/anatomical state rather than the default expression system.

---

## 11. Cartoon rendering

Preferred style:

**Cartoon**

Required qualities:

- clean contours,
- simplified shapes,
- flat colors with light cel shading,
- strong readable silhouette,
- expressive face,
- limited anatomical detail,
- consistent proportions,
- repeatable character generation.

The face should remain readable at small scale.

---

## 12. Color language

The approved reference uses:

- warm humanoid skin tones,
- cream/light exoskeletal plates,
- charcoal/dark exoskeletal elements,
- amber/orange eyes,
- subtle orange accents.

These are examples rather than mandatory colors for every Insectoid.

Different lines and individuals may use different palettes.

The eye structure must remain consistent regardless of color variant:

> **visible white sclera + colored compound iris.**

---

## 13. Visual priority order

If the image generator needs to simplify the design, preserve features in this order:

1. **Humanoid facial proportions**
2. **Large humanoid-shaped eyes with visible white sclera**
3. **Clearly compound/insectoid iris texture**
4. **Simple insectoid mandible structure**
5. **Two simple antennae**
6. **Subtle exoskeletal plates**
7. Minor lineage-specific details

This order is intentionally different from the previous head design.

The humanoid appearance is now the primary visual foundation.

---

## 14. What V2 deliberately avoids

Do not generate the head as:

- a realistic insect head,
- a humanoid head covered in a complete insect carapace,
- a head with fully black/orange compound eyes replacing the sclera,
- a head with no recognizable humanoid facial proportions,
- a head dominated by giant mandibles,
- a head with complex insect mouthparts,
- a head covered with excessive segmentation,
- a realistic arthropod reconstruction.

The biological complexity remains in the world documentation and does not need to be visually exposed.

---

## 15. Definitive visual shorthand

The V2 Insectoid head should be recognizable through:

> **Humanoid face + large almond-shaped eyes with white sclera and compound iris texture + subtle mandibles + two simple antennae + restrained exoskeletal accents.**

The intended first impression should be:

> **“Humanoid character with insectoid traits.”**

not:

> **“Humanoid insect.”**

---

## 16. Scope of V2

This file defines the **approved V2 visual language of the Insectoid head**.

It does not redefine:

- species biology,
- anatomy that is not visually relevant to the head,
- Worker body design,
- Scout body design,
- other specialized lines,
- T-POSE body proportions,
- reproductive or physiological systems.

Those remain governed by their respective worldbuilding documents.

Any future Insectoid character design should use this V2 head as the visual baseline unless a later version explicitly supersedes it.
