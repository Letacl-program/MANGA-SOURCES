## 1. Identity & origin

- Melva is a naturally born Slime with a female humanoid appearance.
- She has no surname.
- She is 25 years old.
- She originated in caves/underground environments.
- She belonged to / came from a close Slime group but lives outside it.
- She is neutral toward humans and other species and judges individuals rather than species.
- She is proud of being Slime.

## 2. Visual identity — T-POSE LOCK

### Base appearance
- Base/default height: **160 cm**.
- Dynamic height range: **160–200 cm**, depending on absorbed fluid.
- Default silhouette: extremely slim/filigree, minimal volume.
- Base bust: approximately **A/minimal** in the project's dynamic Slime scale.
- Bust scale is a Slime volume scale, not a human cup-size measurement.
- Fluid distribution is nonuniform; different body regions may store different amounts independently.
- Hips and bust are major storage regions, but fluid may be redirected anywhere.

### Surface
- Entire body is Slime; there is no separate human-skin material.
- Base color: light bottle green.
- Surface is smooth, extremely glossy, wet-looking, semi-transparent.
- Transparency is moderate: deeper structures can be seen as silhouettes, not fine detail.
- Thicker areas appear optically darker because of thickness/light transmission.
- Extremely thin Slime can become almost colorless with a slight green tint.
- Strong sharp highlights; reflection depends on thickness and lighting.
- Water increases gloss and fluidity.

### Face
- Delicate, slightly triangular face narrowing toward the chin.
- Delicate but clearly defined chin.
- Large classic anime eyes, naturally half-closed/tired.
- Dark bottle-green irises and large dark-green pupils.
- Catchlight is not fixed and follows scene lighting.
- Thin bottle-green Slime eyebrows; highly expressive and able to subtly change shape.
- Default expression: calm, slightly bored.
- Small, subtle anime nose, slightly upturned; narrow frontal view with subtly defined tip.
- Delicate anime mouth with a slightly sassy expression.
- Cheeks are slim but subtly rounder when smiling.
- No separately formed eyelashes; the eye edge is formed by the Slime mass.

### Hair
- Hair is not separate material; it is the same Slime substance as the body.
- Long, slightly wavy, bottle-green mass reaching exactly to the waist.
- One coherent mass, with no distinct parting or separate strands.
- Ends taper slightly and may drip.
- Default hairstyle is stable but consciously reshapeable.
- Hair may flow, drip, merge, spread and reform.

### Ears
- Slime elements in a simplified humanoid auricle shape.
- Usually partly visible; can disappear beneath hair.
- Can independently move/deform and can be completely hidden or reformed.

### Anatomy & proportions
- Standard humanoid arrangement: one head, torso, two arms/hands, two legs/feet.
- All anatomy is Slime and remains fluid/soft.
- Very narrow shoulders and filigree proportions.
- Standard anime head proportion; very thin neck.
- Torso proportional to height; completely flat, very slim belly.
- Slightly rounded hips despite the very slim silhouette.
- Slim back with a gentle natural curve.
- Slim filigree legs with soft fluid transitions.
- Knees and elbows distinct but soft/rounded.
- Upper arms, forearms, thighs and calves slim and proportional.
- Wrists narrow toward the hands.
- Hands small, slim and slightly elongated; palms clearly wider than wrists.
- Five fingers per hand by default, readable but fluid; fingers may merge.
- Feet small, soft and rounded; wider than ankles.
- Five toes per foot, extremely subtle and able to merge.
- Ankles clear but soft/rounded.
- Neck-to-head and neck-to-torso transitions are very fluid.
- Shoulder blades may appear depending on posture/thickness.
- Spine and navel may be consciously formed or erased as surface elements.
- Surface has no fixed patterns; small natural flow differences may appear.

### Integral Slime anatomy
- No human skeleton or organs; body is a continuous Slime mass.
- The core is integral to identity.
- When the legs are only a few centimetres apart, they can merge into one continuous Slime mass while retaining the visual outline of two legs.
- Melva consciously chooses whether the legs merge. She may merge them to increase stability or provide support.
- Arms/hands/feet can merge with adjacent Slime and change shape while retaining function.
- Extra limbs/tendrils/extensions can be created when sufficient mass and control are available.

### Resting posture
- Slightly leaned, lazy stance.
- Head slightly lowered and tilted.
- Both shoulders slightly lowered, with natural asymmetry.
- Weight mostly on one leg.
- Postural asymmetry is freely controlled by Melva.
- Hands hang loosely, fingers nearly touching.

## 3. Hair physical behaviour

- Slight delay behind head movement.
- Ends have greater delay and gently catch up.
- Melva can consciously alter this behaviour.
- Wind makes the hair flow like liquid; intensity depends on physical properties.
- High humidity increases gloss and fluidity.
- Rain runs over the surface; some water is actively absorbed.
- Rain makes the hair heavier, more drooping and potentially partially merged with the body.
- After rain, the prior form returns gradually or faster by conscious choice.
- Swimming spreads hair over the water and may merge it with the surrounding mass.
- After water contact, humanoid shape returns gradually or by conscious reconstruction.
- Fast running produces inertial lag and strong movement.
- Sudden stopping leaves the hair briefly moving/sloshing in the previous direction.
- Sudden head turns produce broad delayed fluid swings.
- Side wind visibly deflects and stretches the whole hair mass.

## 4. Core

- The core is the center of Melva's consciousness, memory and identity.
- Her Slime mass is the executive body, sensory system and carrier of procedural/muscle memory.
- Large, ideal spherical, smooth, pearlescent core.
- Dark pearl colour contrasting with green Slime.
- Floats mainly in the heart region.
- Visible through semi-transparent body but blurred by surrounding thickness.
- In safe conditions it can move within approximately **30 cm**.
- Under threat to the core it may move anywhere within the body mass.
- More resistant than ordinary body Slime, but not indestructible.
- Body regeneration uses fluid/body mass; core regeneration uses absorbed minerals.
- Partial core damage causes strong pain and temporary control disturbance; regeneration temporarily deforms the core and may shift it slightly.
- After regeneration it returns to the heart region and ideal sphere.
- More than **25% permanent core loss/damage** makes full restoration impossible.
- In extreme danger Melva may consciously sacrifice part of the core to save the remainder.
- Complete core destruction causes a brief final loss of consciousness followed by collapse into inert liquid.
- The inert liquid has no consciousness and cannot recreate the original character/core; it can be absorbed or used as material by another Slime/being.
- If all surrounding mass is lost while the core remains intact, Melva is not dead: the core can enter a dormant state and later reactivate in suitable mineral-bearing fluid. In isolation the core is extremely fragile and vulnerable to damage.
- Consciousness, autobiographical memory and identity are centered in the core. The surrounding mass provides the executive body and distributed sensory system rather than being an independent seat of consciousness.
- Partial core damage may cause temporary memory gaps, but identity and recognition of close people remain.
- Severe core damage can cause temporary fogging lasting seconds; basic personality remains unchanged.

## 5. Physical property control

Melva can consciously regulate the following for the whole body or independently by region:
- elasticity/springiness,
- hardness,
- density,
- viscosity,
- adhesion.

Changes affect deformation, mass behaviour, transparency, gloss, reflection, wave/drip behaviour and movement.

Once consciously set, a property remains at that level until Melva changes it.

Density can be changed independently of volume. Multiple properties may be changed simultaneously, but complex combinations require concentration.

Height and bust can vary independently, although they are generally linked. Fluid can be consciously moved between bust, hips and any other region while preserving height.

### Transformation
- Melva can change the form of individual regions or the whole body.
- One region may transform independently if sufficient fluid is available.
- Fluid removed from one area is automatically redistributed/used elsewhere.
- She can intentionally create an asymmetric silhouette, but this requires greater concentration.
- She can restore previous proportions without returning all the way to base.
- She can transform during ordinary movement; transformation precision remains high, though very complex simultaneous tasks increase attention demand.
- Very small changes can be nearly unnoticed.
- She can interrupt a transformation and later continue smoothly.
- She can change the transformation goal only by interrupting the current transformation.
- Short spontaneous transformations may be used to surprise or tease.
- She can use transformations as nonverbal communication.
- If amused by another person's reaction, she may retain or exaggerate a form.
- Larger/more demanding changes require concentration and resources.
- Default appearance is always available.
- The default form is the preferred normal appearance and can return after longer periods unless she consciously keeps another form.

### Remembered forms
- Exactly **6 normal form slots**: fixed default + 5 alternatives.
- Default cannot be deleted/replaced.
- Alternative forms can be saved, selected, replaced or deleted consciously.
- When all alternative slots are occupied, Melva consciously chooses which one to replace.
- Intermediate forms can be saved.
- Switching between remembered forms is smooth.
- She can stop switching at any moment.
- Transformation can be smooth while moving.

## 7. Regeneration & resources

- Regeneration uses stored resources from absorbed fluids and minerals.
- Absorption can occur through any surface.
- Fluid resources can reduce the current form when necessary: bust, hips, hair, overall size or other elements may simplify.
- Regeneration continues while resources are available and may slow when they become scarce.
- Environmental moisture can be used when effective.
- Melva can consciously redirect resources to particular regions.
- Priorities can change at any time.
- Regeneration can be stopped consciously and restarted only when enough resources are available.
- Core protection is normally highest priority, but Melva may choose the resource strategy that best protects the core.
- She can simplify her form to reduce resource costs and later reconstruct the elaborate form.
- Partial regeneration preserves the humanoid silhouette as much as possible.
- When resources return, the previously remembered elaborate form can restore automatically unless she stops it.
- Multiple regions can regenerate simultaneously.
- Regeneration itself is not painful; the returning mass produces relief.
- Heavy regeneration may cause seconds of temporary cognitive fog, but memory and identity remain intact.
- Regeneration after childbirth follows the same normal Slime rules; there is no special postpartum mechanism.

## 8. Absorption & material use

- Melva can consciously allow compatible objects to partially enter her mass.
- She can hold, immobilize, move, surround or pass objects through her body.
- She can use her body as a grip and change adhesion/density.
- She can precisely sense an object's position inside her mass.
- Compatible solid material can be absorbed through a digestion-like process, slower than liquid absorption.
- Useful components/minerals are extracted and retained; incompatible material is isolated.
- She may digest only a selected part of a larger object.
- Digestion can be stopped at any time.
- Unwanted material may be expelled immediately or isolated for later removal.
- Useful component selection is conscious and can require high focus.
- She can temporarily use absorbed material as a structural element.

## 9. Slime fragments

- She can divide her body into fragments down to a minimum of **3 cm³**.
- Fragments share consciousness and sensory input with the main mass.
- She can independently control any number of fragments, within attention/control limits.
- Fragments can execute simple autonomous commands, sequences, conditions, timers, branching and recursion.
- A fragment can change shape, shrink/expand, absorb compatible Slime material and transfer mass.
- Fragments cannot regenerate independently and must return to the main mass for regeneration.
- Maximum direct control range: **15 m**.
- Outside the control range a fragment retains its last simple command.
- If blocked, it can attempt autonomous return; the main mass remains detectable.
- Return behaviour can route around obstacles and learn from experience.
- Experience acquired while separated becomes available after rejoining, but Melva consciously chooses how to use it.
- A fragment has no independent consciousness or identity; its apparent independent behaviour is an extension of the core-centered consciousness and control system.
- Separated fragments can independently use mineral-derived properties according to the minerals already present.
- A separated fragment cannot independently absorb/store minerals for the core system; the main mass must transfer minerals by conscious command/direct contact.
- Thin connected portions preserve full sensation; control precision can decrease with distance/thickness.
- Continuous mass connection is the key to shared control.

## 10. Sensory system

### Touch / temperature
- Touch is human-like but distributed across the whole mass.
- Contact points can be sensed simultaneously without a fixed count limit.
- Water flow around the body can be sensed precisely.
- Ground contact supports touch, pressure and vibration sensing.
- Temperature is primarily sensed as a whole-mass state, with local differences detectable.
- Sudden temperature/humidity changes are noticed immediately.
- Pain can be localized and scales with damage.
- Melva can reduce pain by simplifying/changing form as long as the core remains safe.

### Hearing / vibration
- Ears are decorative; sound is received through the whole body.
- Density can regulate the frequency range she hears.
- She can sense vibrations through air, water, rock, earth and structures.
- Ground/structural vibrations can reveal approaching sufficiently large things before visual contact.
- She can estimate direction, approximate distance and approximate size.
- She can track moving sources and distinguish multiple sources; more sources require more attention.
- Range depends on vibration strength, material and continuity.
- Density can increase range at the cost of precision/attention.
- Contact with ground, walls or water can turn part of her mass into a specialized sensory surface.
- She can distinguish airborne sound from solid/water vibration channels.
- She can detect stationary/moving state, start/stop, direction and approximate tempo.
- Vibration can provide limited spatial mapping in darkness.
- Specific individuals/creatures can be recognized by characteristic vibration patterns.
- She can detect changes such as altered gait or obvious injury/fatigue.
- Structural changes such as cracks, tunnels, chambers and empty spaces can be detected.
- She can actively emit controlled vibrations and analyze returns, including repeated scans.

### Smell / chemoreception
- Smell is approximately human-like; not superhuman.
- The entire surface can recognize basic chemical properties.
- Clear chemical dangers are automatically rejected or isolated.
- She can recognize whether a substance is edible/suitable for use, but does not automatically determine complete nutritional value.
- She can distinguish basic components of mixtures, but not every component or exact proportions.
- She detects only obvious changes over time and obvious traces.
- Characteristic substances can be recognized by smell without contact.
- Clear food spoilage can be detected.
- Only obvious chemical signs of an organism's state can be detected; she cannot diagnose diseases.
- The module is closed at the established level.

### Vision / orientation
- Normal visual perception.
- She sees through her semi-transparent mass and can consciously analyze material structure.
- She maintains spatial orientation independent of humanoid shape.
- After splitting she can determine orientation of the entire connected/fragmented system.

## 11. Automatic reactions & emergency behaviour

- Basic Slime reactions can be learned through repetition or consciously established.
- Several simple automatic patterns may operate simultaneously.
- Simple priorities can be assigned; conflicts invoke conscious control.
- Automatic patterns require some attention.
- Strong core danger can trigger responses faster than conscious choice.
- Core danger automatically triggers shielding/protection and core relocation.
- Melva cannot consciously block the initial automatic core-protection response.
- Protection remains active until consciously ended.
- After ending protection, she remains in the current form until she consciously changes it.
- Severe body damage automatically contracts/limits affected areas.
- Severe core danger may force a simplified Slime form.
- After danger, caution remains until the situation is genuinely calm.
- She remembers lessons from dangerous situations and can use them later.
- Emergency plans may combine form change, movement, splitting and known reactions.
- Plans can contain simple if→then conditions and can be interrupted at any time.
- Core protection is normally the highest priority, but a prepared conscious protection plan can resolve a conflict differently.
- A protection plan for a specific close person is possible.
- Such a plan may shield the person with Melva's mass or extra structures and may include sacrificing her own mass.
- The final decision to sacrifice mass remains Melva's.
- After sacrifice she chooses the order of actions.
- Protection plans can end consciously or automatically when their end condition is met.
- Effectiveness can be evaluated and future plans modified.

## 12. Environment & lifestyle

- Melva does not breathe and can remain submerged indefinitely.
- Water is beneficial but not necessary.
- Fresh water can be absorbed/stored and used to increase volume.
- Salt water supplies useful minerals; useful components are selected and others rejected.
- Extreme cold makes her stiffer/slower; she can change form to reduce the effect.
- Extreme heat makes her more fluid and makes precise humanoid form harder.
- Moderate temperature is preferred.
- She can flatten/squeeze through spaces; the core is the limiting factor.
- Falls can be cushioned by spreading mass, changing elasticity or becoming more fluid.
- Pressure can be spread by flattening or resisted through hardness/density.
- Adhesion can be greatly increased or decreased.
- Walls and ceilings can be traversed by consciously maintaining suitable properties.
- She can run, jump and perform acrobatics normally, using fluidity and property control.
- Sleep is not biologically necessary but is pleasant.
- She can sleep almost anywhere and chooses when to sleep.
- Sudden temperature or humidity changes are particularly effective at waking her.
- Dreams are normally human-like.
- She can eat for pleasure or to obtain specific components but does not require food.
- She has no human metabolism/excretion; unnecessary components are rejected during absorption.
- She can function for long periods from stored resources.
- Resource shortage makes her lethargic and encourages conscious simplification.

## 13. Personality

### Core personality
- Phlegmatic, lazy, perverse in the sense of enjoying ambiguity, teasing and spicy situations.
- Calm and rarely emotionally explosive.
- Enjoys her Slime nature and sometimes deliberately displays it.
- Proud of her variability and enjoys experimenting with her appearance.
- Laziness and overconfidence are weaknesses.
- Humour is ironic, teasing and based on verbal jabs.
- She is curious mainly when something is particularly interesting or spicy.

### Social behaviour
- When provoked she often remains calm and provokes further while pretending indifference.
- Compliments are answered with calm irony or teasing.
- She respects stronger individuals without submissiveness.
- She dislikes unjustified control and excessive seriousness.
- When caught amused she denies it and teases.
- When someone predicts her next transformation, she may comply, mislead or choose based on mood.
- With strangers she usually stops teasing when it clearly crosses a boundary; with friends she may escalate for fun.
- She uses form changes to attract attention subtly and to communicate without words.

### Emotional relationships
- Toward someone she truly likes she becomes more open, affectionate and protective.
- She uses nicknames mainly for people she likes.
- She values trust, closeness and safety.
- When a loved one is sad, she offers affection and a concrete solution.
- If a close person refuses help, she respects the refusal but remains available.
- If she knows she hurt someone close, she apologizes sincerely and follows it with actions.
- Betrayal hurts; she listens and tries to understand before deciding what to do.
- Jealousy is expressed through jokes and boundary-watching.
- Sincere emotional statements make her serious and attentive.
- Public affection is reciprocated, often with pretend embarrassment and humour.
- If told “I feel safe with you”, she reassures and may hug.
- She often denies caring while smiling or becoming more affectionate.
- In genuine love she may initially deny her feelings and rationalize them.
- If love is reciprocated she becomes shy/cautious and lets the other person lead.
- An unexpected hug from a beloved person can make her freeze briefly before she hides embarrassment with a joke.
- “I love you” initially gets a joke, followed by honesty.
- She is tender toward a sleeping loved one and may quietly cuddle.
- A cheek kiss is accepted/reciprocated; embarrassment may be visible through her Slime behaviour.
- Strong comforting embraces may cause her to relax, reciprocate and partly wrap the other person in Slime.

## 14. Reproductive biology — adult Slime / Melva

- Humanoid Slime reproduce with a partner and can become pregnant.
- Reproduction is significantly harder than in humans.
- All adult humanoid Slime have their reproductive period during the **spring quarter**, with individual timing varying by a few days.
- The reproductive period lasts approximately **3 months**.
- The core emits a light glow/sheen during this period.
- Reproductive physiology does not remove consciousness or create a compulsory drive toward any partner.
- A loved or emotionally bonded partner is preferred.
- A suitable partner increases fertilization probability primarily through the emotional bond.
- A very large amount of sperm is required for fertilization.
- Melva's decision to prepare by storing additional fluids/minerals is completely conscious.
- The first core ignition is remembered mainly through emotions and sensations.
- Melva treats an actual reproductive partner as a serious relationship.
- Melva may experience increased spicy/perverse thoughts due to accumulated reproductive stress, especially when without a partner; she retains full consciousness and agency.
- She can partly suppress such thoughts but not necessarily eliminate them.
- She treats the subject as natural and may joke about it.

### Pregnancy
- Pregnancy lasts approximately 3 months, with a small timing tolerance.
- The pregnancy belly grows in jumps due to core growth and accumulation of protective mass.
- After fertilization the core moves to the abdomen and is protected by increased body mass.
- The mother can partially control protective mass distribution and can increase it under danger.
- The developing core has no consciousness before birth.
- The mother senses its basic status, not detailed thoughts or perception.
- The developing core automatically draws minerals from the mother.
- Resource use feels like ordinary fatigue.
- If resources are insufficient, development slows.
- The mother can simplify her own form to free resources.
- She may deliberately accelerate development only to a limited degree; excessive acceleration is risky and can deform the developing core and cause miscarriage.
- The mother can consciously add protective mass around the developing core.
- The developing core can automatically move within the abdomen under threat.
- Normal danger does not automatically trigger premature birth.

### Miscarriage
- Very early termination results in the developing core being absorbed by the mother.
- Miscarriage causes permanent damage to the mother's core.
- A later reproductive period can allow another pregnancy, but any necessary core regeneration must first reach an adequate state.
- Repeated pregnancies/miscarriages can accumulate core damage.
- Accumulated core damage increases miscarriage risk and can shorten lifespan through progressive core degradation.
- Melva retains memories associated with miscarriage.
- Effects on later pregnancies occur through the lasting damage to the mother's core.

### Birth
- Birth timing has a small tolerance rather than occurring at an exact fixed instant.
- Core division is extremely painful, comparable to major core injury.
- The new core is functional immediately but requires later regeneration.
- The mother has limited influence over when birth begins, increased by preparation.
- The mass accumulated during pregnancy and transferred at birth becomes the newborn's initial body mass.
- The newborn is separated from the mother with its own newly divided core.

## 15. New Slime offspring rules relevant to Melva

- A developing/new Slime is always female.
- New Slime is a synthesis of both parents, analogous to inheritance, while remaining Slime.
- The developing core can double its mass and volume.
- The mother's preferred post-birth feeding is her own milk, which supplies minerals in liquid form for rapid core regeneration/development.
- Without the mother, development is slower and the newborn must learn to absorb minerals/fluids.
- Growth/development is approximately 10× faster than a human child.
- Newborn consciousness begins with the functional core and is initially limited in body control.
- The newborn initially recognizes the mother most strongly.
- Parents actively teach body control and language.
- Basic language meanings are understood early; full language is learned gradually.
- Physical and consciousness development proceed at roughly the same pace and depend partly on mineral availability.
- Personality traits can be inherited from both parents.

## 16. Maturity & lifespan

Melva's stated age should be interpreted using the established human/social age convention rather than as a direct biological conversion. Species-level maturation may occur on a much faster internal timescale.

- Sexual maturity occurs at approximately, but not quite, 20 years.
- It is a gradual process with a clear transition at the first reproductive period.
- Before maturity, a young Slime can consciously practise core regeneration.
- Youthful regeneration without reproductive consequences is replaced at maturity by reproductive capability.
- Learning regeneration technique while young improves later control.
- Adults can continue improving regeneration technique, but must accept consequences.
- After adulthood, Slime do not age in the normal human sense.
- Appearance can be consciously maintained as youthful.
- Aging is primarily degradation of mass/core regeneration over time.
- There is no known fixed maximum age.
- Good nutrition, minerals and avoiding excessive core regeneration can allow extremely long life.
- Core damage/regeneration is the major long-term limitation.
- Normal body regeneration without core damage does not degrade the core.
- Regenerating damaged core accelerates degradation.
- Full core regeneration does not guarantee complete rejuvenation.
- Adequate nutrition/minerals slow natural degradation.
- Very old Slime may continue to look young.
- Core degradation may be partially or permanently irreversible depending on age/state.
- Melva's repeated severe core injuries or miscarriages can therefore shorten her lifespan.

## 17. Fusion

Fusion between Melva and a compatible Slime is a distinct state that creates a **new Slime**, not one Slime carrying two consciousnesses.

- Fusion is temporary and requires mutual consent.
- The combined mass is a synthesis of both Slime.
- Both sets of physical properties can be used.
- Both cores remain separate and orbit each other.
- The Fusion has one new combined consciousness.
- Its internal thought/communication is experienced as an internal dialogue reflecting both source personalities.
- Its perception, world model, environmental reactions, humour, preferences and moral judgement are a synthesis.
- It can develop decisions neither parent would independently make.
- It knows it is a new being produced by combination and knows separation will eventually occur.
- Fusion is experienced as a temporary enjoyable “ride”, not something inherently feared.
- Fusion has its own identity and can have a blended name.
- Damage to one core affects the whole Fusion; the other core can assist stabilization.
- Core protection is joint.
- Regeneration during Fusion follows ordinary Slime regeneration, with the two-core configuration as the only special feature.
- Fusion can transform using combined mass and properties.
- Fusion can be long-lasting if resources are sufficient.
- Fusion may end through conscious separation, resource exhaustion, severe damage, critically low mass or threat to both cores.
- Normal separation is initiated by both partners; forced instant separation risks mass loss to the separating Slime.
- Both original Slime remember Fusion experiences after separation.
- Original personalities return after separation, but shared experiences can influence later behaviour.
- Fusion forms cannot occupy normal 5 alternative-form slots.
- Each Slime has a separate list of exactly **3 Fusion form slots**.
- A saved Fusion form can only be reused by fusing again with the same partner.
- Both partners may save the same Fusion form in their own Fusion lists.
- Fusion forms can be replaced/deleted under the same conscious slot-management logic as normal alternative forms.
- Fusion can create forms neither parent previously possessed.
- A Fusion form never becomes Melva's normal base form.

## 19. Design principle

Melva is fundamentally a **humanoid Slime whose identity remains stable while her body is highly adaptable**. Her fluidity is simultaneously biology, movement language, sensory system, practical toolkit and a source of humour.

Her defining contrast is:
**calm, lazy, slightly bored exterior + highly flexible Slime physiology + playful/perverse sense of humour + strong loyalty toward people she truly cares about.**
