## 5. Anatomy and Beastfolk compliance

General rules from `World/races/BEASTFOLK.md`:
- Beastfolk share a humanoid base with two upper and two lower limbs, with patron-specific adaptations.
- Human outer ears are absent; patron-appropriate ears replace them.
- Every Beastfolk has a patron-appropriate tail.
- Natural-form body hair is limited and varies by patron; full-body fur is not universal.
- Hands and feet are generally humanoid in the natural form, though patron traits can be present. Elara's cloven hooves are a character-specific established feature from the supplied description.
- Transformation can alter fur coverage, limbs, face, skeleton, musculature, size, and physiology; such a transformation must be explicitly requested and must not be inferred from a normal outfit/pose change.
- Patron does not determine personality, intelligence, occupation, or magical power.
- Beastfolk cultures differ by community; no universal culture is assumed.
