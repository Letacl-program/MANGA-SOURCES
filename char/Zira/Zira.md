## 6. Goblin anatomy compliance

General rules drawn from `World/races/GOBLIN.md` on branch `World`:
- Goblins are small humanoids with a source-defined height range of 90–120 cm.
- Their build is generally agile, light, and flexible, with a relatively large head, expressive face, and large hands and feet.
- Skin is green and smooth, without default scales, thick folds, or warts.
- Eyes are large and expressive.
- Ears are very long, pointed, and mobile; they may approach the length of the head.
- Small fangs are generally visible when smiling or opening the mouth widely.
- Hands are large with long, dexterous fingers.
- Feet are large but generally human in structure; there are no default animal pads or hooves.
- Goblins have varied hair colors and styles. Male goblins cannot grow a full beard.
- Female goblins are on average shorter and tend to have fuller hips and rounded buttocks; these are tendencies, not rigid rules.
- These general traits do not establish Zira's individual measurements, exact colors, personality, culture, or occupation.
